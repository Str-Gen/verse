# EPUB reader

The reading experience for a local ebook reader with conventional reading and speedreading.

## Language

**Conventional reading**:
Reading book text in its surrounding passage at a self-directed pace.

**Speedreading mode**:
Paced presentation of book text that advances automatically across paragraphs until the reader stops it. Its purpose here is comfortable focus with good comprehension.

**Reading position**:
The place in a book where reading resumes. Returning from speedreading uses the place reached during playback.

**Focus guide**:
A stationary mark beneath playback text that gives the reader a fixed point for visual alignment.

**Display piece**:
The text shown during one playback hold. A vocabulary word can span several display pieces, as with a split hyphenated compound.

**Capture**:
A saved snapshot of recently displayed reading that the reader wants to revisit without pinpointing the distracting word immediately.

**Candidate word**:
A complete word from a capture that may be useful to review. A model suggestion is not confirmation that this word caused difficulty.

**Word list**:
The reader's saved collection of words to revisit, with the captured contexts in which they occurred.
