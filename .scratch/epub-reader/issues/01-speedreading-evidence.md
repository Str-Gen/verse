## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-20 by codex-research-01.

The evidence supports preserving rereading and reader-controlled pacing. RSVP can perform comparably on some comprehension measures at moderate rates, but results vary and higher rates can reduce comprehension. Clause and sentence pauses merit testing; no universal speed, word-length rule, or visual alignment is established. Focus and comfort benefits remain hypotheses to test with this user.

[Speedreading evidence for the EPUB reader](../research/speedreading-evidence.md) records six primary studies, limitations, and proposed prototype comparisons. This resolves the initial evidence investigation; it does not choose final playback behavior or defaults.
